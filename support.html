Common questions

How do I sign in on another phone or computer?

Use the same sign-in method you used first — Apple, Google or your email and password. Your jobs, dates and leave appear automatically.

I didn't get the email to confirm my account

Check your junk or spam folder. On the sign-in screen you can also tap Forgot password? to get a fresh email.

How do I see my calendar in Lifebolt?

In the app, go to You → Calendar and switch on Show my calendar in Jobs, then allow access when your phone asks. You can choose which calendars to include. Events are read on your phone only and are never uploaded. Holiday calendars are left out — Life Clock shows public dates instead.

If you said no to calendar access before, open your phone's Settings, find Lifebolt and allow Calendars.

How do reminders work?

Lifebolt can send a notification at 9am on any day you have jobs due. Switch it on or off in You → Reminders. If notifications are blocked, allow them for Lifebolt in your phone's Settings.

Can I change my life areas?

Yes — go to You → Life areas to rename, recolour, add or remove areas. Dreams is always included.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Go to You → Export your data. Lifebolt saves everything as a file you can keep.
