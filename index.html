Life & work, in one place

One list for everything you juggle: work, home, family and the big dreams. Your meetings sit beside your jobs, and your leave, birthdays and renewals count down on their own.

Free. No adverts, no tracking.

Jobs

Add it in seconds, then forget about it

Type the job, pick its life area and when it's due: today, this week, next week or a date. Repeating jobs bring back the next one when you tick them off.

Switch on calendar sync and your meetings appear between your jobs. Tap one to turn it into a job, like getting ready the day before. Calendar events stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Life Clock

The dates that come round every year

Birthdays, anniversaries, renewals and MOTs, counted down soonest first. Lifebolt adds a reminder job a week before, so the card gets bought and the insurance gets renewed on time.

Add UK or US public dates too, or keep it to just your own.

Leave

Always know how much holiday is left

Set your allowance, the days you work and when your leave year starts. Book holidays and days off, and Lifebolt counts only your working days, so the number left to book is always right.

Dreams

A home for the big things

The trip, the race, the new skill. Dreams stay out of your daily list until you want them. Tap Chase your dreams for a nudge, and mark one achieved when you do it.

Yours, and only yours

Your account keeps everything in step across your phone and the web. Only you can see your data. You can export it or delete it from inside the app whenever you like.
