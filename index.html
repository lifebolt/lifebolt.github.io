Life & work, in one place

One simple list for everything you juggle — work, home and the big dreams — with your leave, birthdays and renewals counting down beside it.

Jobs

Add a job in seconds, pick its life area and when it's due. Repeating jobs add the next one for you when you tick them off.

Dreams

Somewhere for the things you'd love to do one day — kept out of your to-do list until you come looking.

Leave & holidays

Book time off and Lifebolt counts the days you actually work, so you always know how much leave is left.

Life Clock

Birthdays, renewals and anniversaries counting down — with a reminder job before the day comes round.

Your calendar, alongside

On your phone, see your meetings next to your jobs. Calendar events stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Synced and private

Sign in with Apple, Google or email and everything stays in step across your devices. Only you can see your data.
