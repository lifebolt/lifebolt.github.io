Delete your account

You can delete your Lifebolt account and all of its data at any time. This can't be undone.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Lifebolt and go to You.
  2. Optional: tap Export your data first to keep a copy.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete for good.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@lifebolt.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days at the latest.

What gets deleted

Your account and everything in it: jobs and dreams, life areas, Life Clock dates and reminders, leave bookings and settings — on every device. Nothing is kept, except that copies in our database provider's routine backups are overwritten in the normal course of their backup cycle. Calendar events were never stored by Lifebolt, so there's nothing to delete there.
